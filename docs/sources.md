# Activity guidance and illustrations

Reviewed October 22, 2026. Activity wording is a concise paraphrase; the sources do not endorse FreshStep. No treatment outcome is promised. Activity completion is self-reported, with no timer or medical monitoring.

- Breathing: [NHS — Breathing exercises for stress](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/). Comfortable position, gentle unforced breathing, optional counting, about five minutes.
- Stretching: [Mayo Clinic — A guide to basic stretches](https://www.mayoclinic.org/healthy-lifestyle/fitness/in-depth/stretching/art-20546848), shoulder stretch section. Gentle cross-chest shoulder stretch, support away from the elbow, hold about 30 seconds and change sides; do not bounce or stretch into pain.
- Walking: [Smokefree.gov — Fight Cravings with Exercise](https://smokefree.gov/challenges-when-quitting/cravings-triggers/fight-cravings-exercise). Walking and short periods of physical activity are possible alternatives during cravings. Route selection and workplace wording are FreshStep editorial adaptations.

All four SVG illustrations in `assets/` are original artwork created for this project. No third-party image, font, or animation assets are bundled.
