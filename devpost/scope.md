# Quit-Smoking Support Web App

A calm, encouraging web app for taking manageable daily steps toward quitting smoking.

## The Unique Kernel
Pair a clearly labeled daily cigarette count with practical craving support as the count approaches the chosen limit. Celebrate completing a helpful alternative activity so support goes beyond displaying numbers.

## Who It's For
A smoker trying to quit who wants gradual, manageable changes and struggles to find useful resources and alternatives, including activities that fit at a work desk or in a workplace.

## The Core Loop
Open the app, choose a daily cigarette limit, and log cigarettes throughout the day. Check the count against the limit, choose a brief craving-support activity, follow its instructions, and tap “I did it” to receive encouragement and a short confetti celebration. Return throughout the day for tracking and support.

## Inspiration & Identity
Clean, easy to use, modern styling, and motivational nature and fresh-air imagery. Friendly, supportive language that welcomes another attempt and emphasizes small steps. A motivational tagline is desired; final wording remains open. No reference sites supplied.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a short demonstration, set a daily limit of 15 and log cigarettes. Show the circle labeled with the actual count, such as “10 of 15 today,” changing from green at 0–9 to yellow at 10–15 and orange/red above 15. At 10 and above, bring forward friendly craving support. Choose a workplace-friendly activity, follow brief instructions, and tap “I did it” to see “Great work—you’re on your way!” with confetti.

The compelling moment is the transition from tracking to actionable support, followed by encouragement for completing the alternative activity. The demonstration proves the interaction, not a health outcome.

## The POC Boundary
- A web app with a user-chosen daily cigarette limit, cigarette logging, and a clearly labeled colored count circle.
- Friendly craving support brought forward at 10 cigarettes and above in the agreed example.
- A small set of brief, guided activities suitable for a desk or workplace: breathing, gentle stretches or yoga, and a walk where practical.
- An explicit activity-completion action followed by encouraging feedback and a short confetti animation.
- A demonstrable build for a short demo video and public GitHub repository, with deployment retained as the learner’s project goal. Hosting details belong in technical planning.

## Approved Shipping Boundary
Ship the session-only tracking, support, and celebration loop. Reload returns to setup. Persistence, automatic daily reset, and daily check-in are deferred by agreement on October 26, 2026.

## Later
- Money-saved calculations.
- Information about quitting products, including the originally mentioned pouches, gum, and patches; product content would require appropriate research and sourcing.
- A broader medical/health information library.
- A more extensive quit plan and broader healthy-lifestyle features beyond the daily limit and activity loop.

## Explicitly Cut
No additional features were permanently rejected. The larger ideas above are deferred to keep this first experiment small enough to build and demonstrate.

## Questions for Product Definition
Define daily reset and persistence, correcting accidental logs, how support and color thresholds behave for limits other than 15, and the distinction between choosing and completing an activity. These details belong in the PRD rather than expanding the scope.
